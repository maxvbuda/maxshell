## Thomas Edison
aka: edison
Thomas Edison (1847–1931) was an American inventor with over 1,000 patents. He developed the phonograph (the first machine to record and play back sound), a practical long-lasting light bulb, and early film cameras, and he built one of the first industrial research labs, in Menlo Park, New Jersey.
Q: Who invented the light bulb? | Many people worked on it, but Thomas Edison made the first practical, long-lasting one around 1879.

## Nikola Tesla
aka: tesla the inventor
Nikola Tesla (1856–1943) was a Serbian-American inventor and engineer. He developed alternating current (AC) electricity systems — the kind that powers homes today — the induction motor and many ideas about radio and wireless power. The car company Tesla is named after him.

## The Wright brothers
aka: wright brothers, orville and wilbur wright, first airplane
Orville and Wilbur Wright were American brothers who built and flew the first successful powered airplane. On 17 December 1903, at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, Orville flew for 12 seconds and 37 metres. They had taught themselves by studying birds and building gliders and a wind tunnel.
Q: Who invented the airplane? | The Wright brothers, who made the first powered flight in 1903.

## Neil Armstrong
aka: armstrong, first man on the moon
Neil Armstrong (1930–2012) was an American astronaut and the first person to walk on the Moon, on 20 July 1969, during the Apollo 11 mission. As he stepped down he said, "That’s one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind." Buzz Aldrin joined him on the surface.
Q: Who was the first person on the Moon? | Neil Armstrong, on 20 July 1969.

## Amelia Earhart
aka: earhart
Amelia Earhart (1897–1937) was an American aviator, the first woman to fly solo across the Atlantic Ocean, in 1932. She inspired many people, especially women, to fly. In 1937 she disappeared over the Pacific while trying to fly around the world, and what happened is still a mystery.

## Ludwig van Beethoven
aka: beethoven
Ludwig van Beethoven (1770–1827) was a German composer and pianist, one of the greatest in history. He wrote nine symphonies, including the famous Fifth and the Ninth with its "Ode to Joy", plus piano sonatas and concertos. Amazingly, he kept composing after he became almost completely deaf.

## Vincent van Gogh
aka: van gogh
Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch painter known for bold colours and swirling brushstrokes, in works like The Starry Night and Sunflowers. He sold very few paintings in his lifetime and struggled with mental illness, but he is now one of the most famous artists ever.

## Pablo Picasso
aka: picasso
Pablo Picasso (1881–1973) was a Spanish artist who helped invent Cubism, a style that shows objects from several angles at once. Among his best-known works is Guernica, a powerful painting about the horror of war. He made tens of thousands of paintings, drawings and sculptures.

## Frida Kahlo
aka: kahlo, frida
Frida Kahlo (1907–1954) was a Mexican painter famous for her vivid self-portraits, which draw on Mexican culture and her own life, including the pain from a bus accident she survived as a teenager. She is a symbol of Mexican identity and of strength.

## Steve Jobs
aka: jobs
Steve Jobs (1955–2011) co-founded Apple with Steve Wozniak in 1976, in a garage. He led the creation of the Macintosh, the iPod, the iPhone and the iPad, and was known for caring intensely about design and simplicity. He also ran Pixar, which made Toy Story.

## Bill Gates
aka: gates
Bill Gates (born 1955) co-founded Microsoft with Paul Allen in 1975. Microsoft’s Windows and Office became the most used software on personal computers, making Gates one of the richest people in the world. Later he focused on his foundation, which works on global health and education.

## Tim Berners-Lee
aka: berners-lee, inventor of the web
Tim Berners-Lee (born 1955) is an English computer scientist who invented the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. He created the first web browser, web server and the ideas of URLs, HTTP and HTML — and gave them away for free so anyone could use them.

## Cleopatra
aka: cleopatra
Cleopatra VII (69–30 BCE) was the last active ruler of ancient Egypt’s Ptolemaic kingdom. She was clever and spoke many languages, and allied with the Roman leaders Julius Caesar and Mark Antony. After their defeat by Octavian, Egypt became part of the Roman Empire.

## Julius Caesar
aka: caesar
Julius Caesar (100–44 BCE) was a Roman general and statesman. He conquered Gaul (much of modern France), won a civil war and became dictator of Rome. He was assassinated on the Ides of March (15 March) 44 BCE by senators who feared his power. The month of July is named after him.

## Napoleon Bonaparte
aka: napoleon
Napoleon Bonaparte (1769–1821) was a French military leader who became Emperor of France. He conquered much of Europe in the early 1800s and created the Napoleonic Code, a set of laws still influential today. He was finally defeated at the Battle of Waterloo in 1815 and spent his last years in exile on the island of Saint Helena.

## George Washington
aka: washington
George Washington (1732–1799) led the American army in the Revolutionary War against Britain and became the first president of the United States, from 1789 to 1797. He chose to step down after two terms, setting an example of peaceful handover of power.
Q: Who was the first president of the United States? | George Washington, from 1789 to 1797.

## Stephen Hawking
aka: hawking
Stephen Hawking (1942–2018) was an English physicist who studied black holes and the origin of the universe. He showed that black holes should give off radiation, now called Hawking radiation. He wrote the bestseller A Brief History of Time, and did his work despite being paralysed by motor neurone disease.

## Jane Goodall
aka: goodall
Jane Goodall (born 1934) is an English scientist famous for studying wild chimpanzees in Tanzania for over 60 years. She discovered that chimpanzees make and use tools and have complex social lives and personalities, and she has worked all her life to protect animals and the environment.
